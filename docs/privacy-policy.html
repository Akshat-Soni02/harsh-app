Privacy Policy for Pragti: Routine & Rehab Companion

Last Updated: November 2024

We value your trust and are deeply committed to protecting your family's and patients' privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how our mobile application handles information, user data, and device permissions.

🔒 100% Local-First & Private: All routines, photos, family voice notes, and progress logs are saved exclusively on your local device. We do not sell, rent, track, or share your personal information or health data with third-party advertisers or external servers.

1. Information We Handle

The Application is designed to operate primarily offline without requiring personal data transmission. The types of data stored locally on your device include:

  • Routine and Exercise Data: Exercise titles, target repetition counts, sets, duration, and targeted muscle/body parts.
  • Voice Guidance Audio: Short voice clips recorded by parents or therapists to guide individuals through exercises.
  • Visual Reference & Progress Photos: Photos captured with the camera or selected from your gallery to visually illustrate proper exercise postures or track long-term recovery.
  • Completion & Consistency Logs: Timestamps and day counts used to generate daily streaks and monthly consistency charts.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To deliver essential routine and physical therapy features, the application requests the following device permissions:

🎤 Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)
Used strictly when a parent, guardian, or therapist voluntarily records a personal audio prompt to coach the user during their exercises. Recordings stay on the device.
📷 Camera (CAMERA)
Used solely to take exercise reference photos (e.g., showing proper limb placement) or progress check-in photos. Photos are stored locally in the app's secure sandbox.
🖼️ Media & Photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
Allows you to select an existing photo from your device's photo gallery as a reference posture for an exercise.
⏰ Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)
Used strictly to trigger user-configured daily routine reminders and alarm ringtones at scheduled times (e.g., morning or evening exercise sessions).

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Our application does not include third-party advertising networks, data brokers, tracking SDKs, or behavioural profiling tools. Your health, routine, and rehabilitation progress remains completely private between you, your family, and your healthcare provider.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The application is built to be a gentle, supportive routine companion suitable for individuals of all ages, including children with special physical therapy needs. Routine setup and configuration is protected under Parent Mode.

We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or request personal personally identifiable information (PII) from children. All content remains under parental control on the family device.

5. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete individual exercises, recorded voice notes, photos, or routine sessions at any time from Parent Mode.
  • Device Backups: The app offers an export feature that compresses your routines and records into a local `.zip` file for safe migration to a new device.
  • Complete Erasure: Clearing the app's storage in Android Device Settings or uninstalling the app permanently purges 100% of data stored on the device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new capabilities (such as optional cloud backup or therapist synchronization). Any updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
